FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers about Vault Movement — formerly Chennai Parkour — our studios, programmes, Vault Kids, the Vault Method and how to get started.

About Vault Movement

Is Vault Movement the same as Chennai Parkour?

Yes. Chennai Parkour, founded in Chennai in 2008, is now Vault Movement. Same coaches, same safety-first method — a new name for where we are going.

Where are your studios, and what are your hours?

City Studio · Ekkattuthangal and Coast Studio · Injambakkam, ECR. Both are open every day, 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM.

How much does it cost?

Every journey starts with an Experience Session. We share plans after it, on a call or on WhatsApp.

Do you offer franchises?

Yes. We welcome franchise enquiries — see our Franchise page.

Programmes

What is Vault 12, and how is it different from Movement & Strength?

Vault 12 is a 12-week, coach-led starter programme with a structured Monday–Friday Gameplan and a nutrition plan. Movement & Strength comes next: six days a week, sixty-minute sessions on two alternating Gameplans, progressing through Vault One, Two and Three.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes. Personal Training runs as PT Trio (a group of three) or PT 1:1, on a Gameplan built around you.

What is the My Vault app?

The member app for your Gameplans, progress, level badges and nutrition plan. Members log in at app.vaultmovement.fit.

Are there levels?

Yes. The Vault Method has five coach-verified levels, Vault One to Vault Five. Your coach verifies every movement live, and you earn a level when every movement in it is verified.

What happens in a Movement & Strength session?

Movement skills after a warm-up, then your strength workout, then a flexibility and mobility routine.

What is calisthenics?

Strength training that uses your own bodyweight as resistance, from your first push-up, pull-up or squat to dips, muscle-ups and handstands. We use progressive calisthenics to build strength, mobility, coordination and body control, whether you are a complete beginner or already strong.

Is weight training required?

Not at the start. Most of the work uses your own bodyweight. Beginners learn good form on bodyweight movements for a few weeks before free weights are added for more strength and power.

Is parkour still part of Vault Movement?

Yes. The fundamentals — balance, landing and safe falling, running and jumps, vaults, swings and climbs — are part of Movement & Strength, taught progressively at ground level.

Getting started

What level of fitness do I need to start?

None. People of all fitness levels are welcome — lean or heavy, short or tall, or feeling weak. We start with the basics, within your comfort zone, and help you build from there, with a community that keeps you going.

Should I lose weight or get stronger before I join?

Not at all. There are movements for every body type, and your coach builds your programme around where you are today.

Am I too old to start?

No. Our members range from toddlers, who train with a parent, to adults in their sixties and beyond. It is never too late to start moving.

Vault Kids

What age can kids start?

Vault Kids runs Toddler, Under 10 and Under 14 batches. Parents are welcome to join in at Toddler sessions.

Do you work with neurodivergent children?

Yes. Adaptive Movement is for autistic children, children with ADHD and children with additional needs, in small groups or 1:1. It complements your child's therapy, never replaces it.

How do you keep children safe?

A higher coach-to-child ratio, age-appropriate equipment and mats, and regular parent updates. No child moves to a new skill until the prerequisite is solid.

Safety and training

Is it safe?

Yes, when you train with a coach through the right progressions and conditioning. We teach on padded surfaces, so you can learn without fear, and processing fear in stages is one of the first skills you learn.

Do you train at ground level?

Yes. We do not teach or encourage training anywhere with a risk of serious injury. Most experienced movers train at ground level, and there is plenty of challenge and fun in safe, ground-level training.

Does movement training help concentration?

Yes. Every movement needs focus. Beginners practise landing and balance drills that build precision, control and concentration.

Will it help my other sports?

Yes. Whether it's football, tennis, cricket or badminton, movement training builds agility, mobility and spatial awareness that carry into your sport.
