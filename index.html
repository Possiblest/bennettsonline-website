FAQ

Questions, answered.

All FAQs
Is Vault Movement the same as Chennai Parkour?

Yes. Chennai Parkour, founded in Chennai in 2008, is now Vault Movement. Same coaches, same safety-first method — a new name for where we are going.

Where are your studios, and what are your hours?

We have two studios in Chennai: City Studio · Ekkattuthangal and Coast Studio · Injambakkam, ECR. Both are open every day, 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM.

How much does it cost?

Every journey starts with an Experience Session. We share plans after it, on a call or on WhatsApp.

I'm new to fitness. How do I begin?

Book an Experience Session. Your coach assesses your strength, mobility, coordination and movement patterns, and matches you to a programme — for most people new to fitness, that is Vault 12, our 12-week coach-led starter.

What age can kids start?

Vault Kids runs Toddler, Under 10 and Under 14 batches. Parents are welcome to join in at Toddler sessions.
