Movement & Strength

Strength becomes skill. Sixty-minute sessions of movement, calisthenics and strength, coached and verified level by level.

Movement & Strength is Vault Movement's core adult programme in Chennai. It combines parkour fundamentals, calisthenics and strength training in 60-minute coach-led sessions, up to six days a week, at our City Studio (Ekkattuthangal) and Coast Studio (Injambakkam, ECR).

What you train

What will I train in Movement & Strength?

  • Land, balance, fall safely

    Balance lines and bars, soft landings, floor connectors and break-falls. The foundation everything else is built on.

  • Run and jump

    Running mechanics, jump technique, precision and control. Then: stick the landing.

  • Vault

    Leaping and diving vaults over boxes and rails, linked into flowing combo lines.

  • Swing and climb

    Bar swings, traverses, cat hangs, climb-ups and wall runs. Grip, pull and whole-body control.

  • Calisthenics skills

    Inversions, levers, planche progressions and the muscle-up, unlocked as your strength allows.

  • Strength, conditioning, mobility

    Pull, push, hinge and squat, single-leg work, core and cardio, finished with pike, shoulder, back-bridge and ankle mobility.

Every movement is progressed from the ground up, on mats, by your coach.

FAQ

Movement & Strength questions.

All FAQs
Do I need to finish Vault 12 first?

No. If you're already active, your baseline at the Experience Session may place you straight into Movement & Strength.

Is this parkour?

Parkour fundamentals — vaults, jumps and safe landings — are a big part of it, trained at ground level on mats, alongside calisthenics and strength.

Do I have to come six days a week?

Six days are available. Your coach will recommend how many suit your goal and recovery. Minimum days Confirm

How do I level up?

Your coach verifies each movement live. When every movement in a level is verified, the level is yours, recorded in My Vault.

I'm over 40. Can I join?

Yes. Progressions start where your body is today. Our members range from teenagers to people in their 60s.
