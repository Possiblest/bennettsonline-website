Personal Training

Your goal. Your Gameplan. Your coach — in a trio or one-to-one.

Vault Movement offers personal training in Chennai in two formats: PT Trio (one coach, three people) and PT 1:1. Both start with a baseline assessment and run on a personal Gameplan tracked in the My Vault app.

Two ways to train

PT Trio or PT 1:1?

PT Trio

Train as three.

One coach, three people, one Gameplan shaped around your shared goal and adjusted for each of you. Personal attention, with the energy of training together.

Best for: friends, partners, family or colleagues who want to train together.

Can we match individuals into a trio? Confirm

PT 1:1

All of your coach. All of the session.

A fully personal Gameplan, at your pace, on your schedule.

Best for: a specific goal or deadline · privacy · returning after a break (with your doctor's clearance if recovering from an injury) · mastering a specific skill · training toward Vault Four and coaching.

What's included

What do both formats include?

  • Baseline assessment and goal-setting

  • A personal Gameplan in your My Vault app

  • Coach-verified progress and level-ups

  • Regular check-ins and goal reviews

  • Nutrition guidance Confirm

  • Train at either studio, 6 AM – 9 PM, every day
