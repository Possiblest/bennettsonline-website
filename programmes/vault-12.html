Vault 12

A 12-week, coach-led starter transformation.

Vault 12 is a 12-week, coach-led starter programme at Vault Movement, a Fitness & Lifestyle Studio. It builds basic fitness, sustainable weight loss and strength through small-group coaching, a structured weekly Gameplan and a nutrition plan from our nutritionist. It is built for people new to fitness, getting back to form, or training without progress.

Who it's for

Is Vault 12 right for me?

If one of these sounds like you, Vault 12 is a good place to start.

  • You want sustainable weight loss

  • You need accountability

  • You're getting back to form

  • You have an important event coming up

  • You already work out but see no progress

  • Your doctor has advised you to lose weight to protect your knees

Vault 12 FAQ

Questions about Vault 12.

All FAQs
What is Vault 12, and how is it different from Movement & Strength?

Vault 12 is a 12-week, coach-led starter programme: a structured Monday–Friday Gameplan, small-group coaching and a nutrition plan. Movement & Strength comes next — six days a week, sixty-minute sessions on two alternating Gameplans, progressing through Vault One, Two and Three.

Do I need to be fit to start?

No. Vault 12 is built for people new to fitness or getting back to form. Your Experience Session sets your baseline first.

Will I have to give up my favourite food?

Lose weight, not your favourite food. Vault 12 includes a nutrition consultation and a nutrition plan from our nutritionist, built for sustainable habits.

My doctor has advised me to lose weight. Can I join?

Yes, with your doctor's clearance. Share it at your Experience Session so your coach can plan around it.

How much does Vault 12 cost?

Every journey starts with an Experience Session. We share plans after it, on a call or on WhatsApp.

What happens after the 12 weeks?

The next step is Movement & Strength, where you progress through Vault One, Two and Three, verified by your coach.
