Privacy Policy

How Vault Movement collects, uses and protects your personal data.

Draft for legal review. Items marked Confirm need the owner's input.

Who we are

Vault Movement Pvt Ltd (“Vault Movement”, “we”), formerly Chennai Parkour, runs this website and our studios in Chennai. Contact: +91 99626 96859 · email Confirm.

What we collect

  • Details you send through our enquiry forms, which run on Zoho Forms: your name, phone number, email (optional), programme, studio and message.
  • For Vault Kids enquiries: a parent or guardian's details, the child's first name and batch only. We do not ask for a child's date of birth or photos on this website.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • How you arrived at the site (for example, campaign tags in the link), recorded with your enquiry.

Cookies and analytics

Essential storage keeps the site working and remembers your cookie choice. Google Analytics 4 and the Meta Pixel load only if you choose “Accept all”. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Embedded content

Instagram reels and Google Maps load only when you tap them. When they load, Instagram or Google may collect data under their own policies.

How we use your data

To reply to your enquiry, book your Experience Session, share plans, and run and improve our programmes. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Service providers who help us run the website and enquiries, such as Zoho (forms), Meta (WhatsApp) and Google — and no one else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Retention period Confirm.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Contact us at +91 99626 96859 or email Confirm.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
