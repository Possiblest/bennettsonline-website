Your Journey

From your first move to mastery

Every Vault Movement journey starts with an Experience Session, where a Movement Coach assesses your baseline and matches you to a programme. From there you level up through coach-led programmes, with your progress verified level by level.

    Step 01: Experience Session

    A baseline assessment of your strength, mobility, coordination and movement patterns; a consultation with a Movement Coach; and a programme match.

Verified progress

How is progress verified?

Your coach unlocks a movement as part of your Gameplan, you train together, you achieve the Level-Up Target, and your coach verifies it live. Every level-up is recorded in the My Vault app, where you see your Gameplan and progress.

FAQ

Journey questions.

All FAQs
How long does each stage take?

Vault 12 runs for twelve weeks. After that there is no fixed timeline: you and your coach decide when you are ready for the next step, based on your goal and verified progress — not the calendar.

Can I skip Vault 12?

Yes, if your baseline shows you are ready. Already-active adults may be placed straight into Movement & Strength after their Experience Session, and Personal Training can start right after it.

Can I switch paths?

Yes. Your path follows your goal. Talk to your coach about moving between programmes — for example, from Movement & Strength to Personal Training.

Is it suitable at my age or fitness level?

Yes. All ages and fitness levels are welcome. Your Experience Session starts from where your body is today, and every programme is coach-led and safety-first.
