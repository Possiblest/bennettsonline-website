Vault Kids

Born to Play. Built to Move.

Vault Kids is Vault Movement's programme for children, in Toddler, Under 10 and Under 14 batches. Screen-free, joy-first sessions build coordination, balance, strength, confidence, focus and real friendships.

What kids build

What will my child get from Vault Kids?

Screen-free, joy-first sessions where children move, play and build real physical confidence.

  • 01

    Coordination

    Hands, feet and eyes working together.

  • 02

    Balance

    Steady on a line, soft and quiet on landing.

  • 03

    Strength

    Crawling, pushing and bridging with their own bodyweight.

  • 04

    Confidence

    Trying something new, one safe step at a time.

  • 05

    Focus

    Listening, taking turns and finishing a move well.

  • 06

    Real friendships

    Moving together and cheering each other on.

Safety

How do you keep children safe?

Safety-first coaching is how we have worked since 2008.

  • A higher coach-to-child ratio

  • Age-appropriate equipment and mats

  • No child moves to a new skill until the prerequisite is solid

  • Regular parent updates

Vault One moves, explained for kids.

Each move comes in three simple steps, one funny mistake to avoid, and a “Coach says” safety line. Ground-safe moves are marked Try at home.

  • Soft, quiet landing
  • Dismount

All FAQs
What age can kids start?

Vault Kids runs Toddler, Under 10 and Under 14 batches. Parents are welcome to join in at Toddler sessions.

How do you keep children safe?

A higher coach-to-child ratio, age-appropriate equipment and mats, and regular parent updates. No child moves to a new skill until the prerequisite is solid.

Do you work with neurodivergent children?

Yes. Adaptive Movement is for autistic children, children with ADHD and children with additional needs, in small groups or 1:1, with close parent collaboration. It complements your child's therapy, never replaces it.

Do you host birthday parties?

Yes. Birthdays & Events are movement parties where children play under the guidance of our coaches.

How much does it cost?

Every journey starts with an Experience Session. We share plans after it, on a call or on WhatsApp.
