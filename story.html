Our story

The story behind the name.

Every great practice needs a place to keep what it learns. Since 2008, our coaches have collected, tested and refined the ways the human body moves — landing, balancing, crawling, climbing, lifting, vaulting. That collection became our Vault.

Three meanings

Why Vault?

  1. 01

    A vault of movement

    A library of skills, built over eighteen years and unlocked one level at a time.

  2. 02

    Vaulting

    One of the oldest human movements — hands down, and through the obstacle.

  3. 03

    The V

    Every founding partner's first name begins with V. The mark carries that initial.

The mark

A V breaking through a circle.

The circle represents every pattern you are ready to outgrow. The V represents your first move that quantum leaps your transformation!

Transformation is not one leap. It is choosing to move differently, again and again, until a new pattern takes hold and you move freely.

We Move

From Vault Movement to We Move.

Take “ault” from Vault and “ment” from Movement and you are left with V Move. Say it aloud and it becomes Vee & Mouve — and, in the end, We Move.

Vee and Mouve are our two movers on the Vault Kids pages.

Formerly Chennai Parkour

Why did the name change?

We grew beyond one city and one discipline. Same coaches, same safety-first method — and a name for where we are going.
