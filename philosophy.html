Our philosophy

Five principles every session is built on.

Vault Movement's coaching rests on five principles: natural movement, play, fear metabolism, belonging and purpose. Here is what each one means in practice, with the research behind it.

  1. 01 · Natural Movement

    The body was made to move all day, not for one intense hour.

    24/7 · Not one hour

    We train the movements you use in life — walking, crawling, climbing, carrying and landing — so moving well becomes part of your day. Physical activity is linked to a lower allostatic load,1 and a high allostatic load predicts frailty.2

  2. 02 · Play

    Play is not a reward. It is essential.

    Joy-first

    Every session leaves room for curiosity and play — for adults as much as kids.3

  3. 03 · Fear Metabolism

    Most people never learn to move through fear. We teach it, one safe step at a time.

    One safe step at a time

    We break every movement into safe steps, on padded surfaces and at ground level, so confidence builds one success at a time.4

  4. 04 · Belonging

    The people who live longest belong somewhere.

    +50% survival odds with strong social bonds

    Strong social bonds are linked to 50% better survival odds, and loneliness is comparable to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day.5,6 Our studios are communities, not just class timetables.

  5. 05 · Purpose / Ikigai

    People with a reason to get up every morning live longer — and stay capable longer.

    31% lower risk of functional disability

    A sense of purpose is linked to a 31% lower risk of functional disability.7 A practice that grows with you for decades is a reason to keep showing up.

We are not just building athletes. We are building humans who age differently.

Five principles. Every session.
