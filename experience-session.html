Every journey starts here

Experience Session

An Experience Session is your first visit to Vault Movement: a baseline assessment, a consultation with a Movement Coach and a programme match. It is for adults and kids, at City Studio · Ekkattuthangal and Coast Studio · Injambakkam, ECR.

Book nowWhatsApp usCall us

What happens

What happens in an Experience Session?

  1. 01

    Baseline assessment

    Your coach looks at your strength, mobility, coordination and movement patterns.

  2. 02

    Consultation with a Movement Coach

    We talk about what you want from your body — your goal, your history and your week.

  3. 03

    Programme match

    You leave with clarity, and the right programme for you.

Who it's for

Adults and kids

FAQ

Before you book.

All FAQs
What level of fitness do I need?

None. People of all fitness levels are welcome — lean or heavy, short or tall, or feeling weak. We start with the basics, within your comfort zone, and help you build from there.

Should I lose weight or get stronger before I start?

Not at all. There are movements for every body type, and your coach builds your programme around where you are today.

How much does it cost?

Every journey starts with an Experience Session. We share plans after it, on a call or on WhatsApp.

Can I book for my child?

Yes. Kids start with an Experience Session too, and their coach places them in the right batch: Toddlers, Under 10 or Under 14.
