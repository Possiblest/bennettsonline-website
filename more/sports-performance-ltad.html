Performance training

Sports Performance & LTAD

Sports Performance at Vault Movement is periodised, measurable training for competitive athletes and sports teams: explosive jumps, sprint mechanics, throwing power, agility, endurance and movement control. Our long-term athlete development (LTAD) work builds young athletes' foundations in the right order.

LTAD

What is long-term athlete development?

Long-term athlete development (LTAD) builds a young athlete's movement, strength and speed in the right order for their stage of development, so performance is built on solid foundations.

All FAQs
Will movement training help my sport?

Yes. Whether it's football, tennis, cricket or badminton, movement training builds agility, mobility and spatial awareness that carry into your sport.

Do you work with teams?

Yes. Training cycles are built for individuals and for teams.
